🧪 = experimental: it works in our tests but hasn't had wide testing on real hardware yet - reports welcome.

## ✨ New
- **Online mods** 🧪: superstars, move packs, arenas and backstage mods travel to the other players in a lobby. You're asked first, see a progress bar, and the mods are only kept for that session - they never end up in your save.
- **Report a problem** (launcher and Mod Maker): sends the problem straight to us, with the last match and the mods it used.
- **Match recordings**: each match is recorded (who, where, which mods, the inputs) so a report can show exactly what happened.
- **CPU difficulty** (OPTIONS > CPU): reversal chance, damage, pin and momentum levels, and a LEGEND+ preset.
- **Resolutions up to 8K** from your screen's own modes, and AA that really smooths the picture now.
- **Road to WrestleMania - VS. Undertaker** 🧪: Sort > MODS lists your superstar mods (it showed only your CAW when the CAW wasn't in the first slot), and the info box shows the mod. The mod plays the story's matches; the backstage walks keep your CAW.
- **Installing a mod with custom moves** now warns you first: ported moves are experimental and often land wrong or look odd.
- **Community Creations name filter**: uploads with slurs or hate names are refused, with a clear message.
- **The SvR 2008 / 2009 / 2010 VS screens**, for the whole game or per arena mod (Mod Maker > Arena > VS screen).
- **Legends of WrestleMania**: its superstars and arenas (WrestleMania I-XV, Royal Rumble 2008) in the Mod Maker 🧪.
- **Bigger mods**: six big models in a 6-man, arenas bigger than the stock size, HCTP worlds with all six rooms.
- **Victory scenes** for superstar and attire mods, from SvR 2008-2010 / LoWM too.
- **Superstar mods carry their own loading picture.**
- **Crowd weapons** 🧪: taunt at the barricade in a crowd arena in Extreme Rules and the fans hand you a weapon.
- **Mod Maker**:
  - **Move preview, inspector and "Will it work in-game?"** check, with a report you can export.
  - Ported dives land properly - Axe Handle, Corkscrew and Springboard Moonsault (port the move again).
  - Movies are made and carried as `.mp4` (much smaller); the launcher can shrink big movies.
  - Add / remove / export objects in YOBJ models; multi-select in the Materials tab; ring announcer volume.
  - PS2 SvR 2011 superstars; WWE 2K17 SummerSlam and more 2K arenas with their names and pictures.
- **Discovery**: download progress with Cancel and resume; owners can edit, upload a new version or remove their own mods.
- Android: custom touch-control icons (thanks **Gabpogi2125**), smoother performance on phones that support Game Mode.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- **"Can't start match due to move pack"** online, even with Start Without Mods and every mod off - fixed (the lobby waited for a list that never came after the first online match).
- **Inviting a friend after an online match** didn't work - fixed.
- **A cut-off settings file reset every setting** - now only the broken line is set aside.
- **A load that ran out of game memory hung** - it now says so and closes with a crash report.
- **USER MOVIES**: the last page showed the previous page's rows - fixed.
- **Mod grapples froze** the match - fixed.
- **Quitting an Extreme Rules match** with extra weapons crashed or hung - fixed.
- **Road to WrestleMania**: Cena's week 2 MVP crash - fixed.
- **A mod swapped into a slot kept the old mod's entrance and movie** - fixed.
- **Ported arenas**: no more T-posed announcer; WWE '12 minitrons play.
- **HCTP backstage** 🧪 (beta mods): climbs, gates and exits work better.
- **Bundled mods install switched off** - turn on the ones you want in Installed Mods.
- Ring mats' light patches evened out (GRAPHICS > RING MAT).
