# Privacy Policy

*WWE SmackDown vs. Raw 2011 PC port (unofficial fan project) · last updated 30 September 2026*

The port does not collect, store or sell personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.

## What stays on your device

Settings, saves, achievements, logs and crash reports are files in the game folder on your own PC or phone. Nothing is uploaded.

## Network use

- **Update check (launcher):** the launcher asks GitHub (`api.github.com`) for the latest release of this project and downloads updates from GitHub when you choose to. GitHub's own [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) applies to those requests.
- **Android USB install (launcher):** if adb is missing, the launcher can download Google's platform-tools, with your permission, from Google's servers.
- **Discord Rich Presence (Windows):** while the game runs, it tells the Discord app on your own computer that you are playing, what you are doing (in the menus, watching the entrances, in a match) and for how long. This is sent only to your local Discord client, which shows it on your profile according to your Discord settings. Turn it off with `discord_presence = false` in `svr2011.toml`, or Discord's Activity Privacy settings. [Discord's privacy policy](https://discord.com/privacy) applies to what Discord does with it.
- The game has no online play (Xbox LIVE features are disabled).
